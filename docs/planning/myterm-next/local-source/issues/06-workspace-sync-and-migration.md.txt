## Question

How do servers own shared workspace structure, panes and tabs while each client retains independent navigation and display preferences? Decide concurrent structural edits, authoritative persistence, identifiers, offline/stale-client behavior and server switching. Define how existing workspace files are recognized, backed up, migrated and validated without losing workspaces, even though devices may re-enroll and old wire protocols may be retired.
