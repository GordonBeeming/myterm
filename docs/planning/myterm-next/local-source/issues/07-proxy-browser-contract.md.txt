## Question

What browser behavior must the proxy-only architecture support consistently on Mac, iPhone and iPad clients against every native server platform? Decide server-local/private destination routing, origin and TLS behavior, cookies/authentication, WebSockets, browser history and per-client browser state, local-file expectations, and destination authorization. Remove dependence on Mac-rendered streaming. Use concrete local development and login scenarios to settle feature parity and limits.
