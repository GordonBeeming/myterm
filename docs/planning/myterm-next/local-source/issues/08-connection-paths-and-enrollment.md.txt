## Question

How should local automatic startup, direct hostname/IP connections and relay connections identify and authenticate the same server? Decide discovery, explicit connection selection or failover, relay selection, first-device trust, unattended server registration, enrollment credential lifetime and cloning/revocation behavior. Include Azure VM scale-set startup and machine replacement. Newly enrolled servers must become available to approved clients without a separate QR scan per server.
