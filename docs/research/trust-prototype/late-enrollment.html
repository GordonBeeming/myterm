An enrollment first seen after expiry

Servers already recognized by the account keep their membership when their enrollment credential expires or is revoked. The unresolved case is a server whose enrollment has never been independently verified.

Agreed rule

Require a fresh credential or approval from a current trusted endpoint for previously unseen enrollment first presented after its credential expires or is revoked. Already recognized servers remain enrolled.

This is an explicit exception to the earlier no-cascade rule: a legitimate server enrolled offline may need approval again. Its local sessions remain owned by that server. Offline enrollment can still complete while the credential is valid.

The alternative is to accept the late unseen record and its claimed earlier date. A holder retaining the expired credential can then manufacture a new record with the same kind of evidence. A claimed timestamp signed by that holder does not independently establish issuance time.
